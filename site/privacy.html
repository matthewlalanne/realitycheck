REALITY CHECK PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated September 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, display name, and profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name, email, and profile photo, and nothing else from your Google account.
  • League activity: leagues you create or join, your draft picks, weekly picks, and standings.
  • Photos you upload: profile pictures and castaway photos you choose to add.
  • Notifications: a device token, only if you turn notifications on.

How we use it

To run your leagues: sign you in, show your name and picks to the other members of your league, calculate scores, and send the reminders you opt into. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Who else handles it

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, storage, hosting).
  • Resend, which delivers sign-in code emails.
  • Google, if you choose Google sign-in.

Who can see it

Other members of a league you join can see your display name, photo, and picks. Nobody outside your league can.

Deleting your data

Email lalanne.matthew@gmail.com and we will delete your account and its data. Photo takedown requests are handled the same way.

Children

Reality Check is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes.
